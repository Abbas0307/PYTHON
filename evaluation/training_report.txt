1. HISTORICAL CONTEXT & PRELIMINARY EXPERIMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Preliminary Experiment 1 (84.11% Acc): Included 'Have you ever had suicidal thoughts?'.
  -> Result: Overfitted on crisis symptom (~60% split importance). Inappropriate for lifestyle screening.
Preliminary Experiment 2 (80.12% Acc): Included 'Family History of Mental Illness'.
  -> Result: Discarded to respect student privacy and avoid unverified family-health assumptions.
Current Production Pipeline: Strictly uses 10 non-leaking, educational lifestyle & study features.

2. EXPERIMENT OVERVIEW & METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------
Target Variable: Self-reported Depression indicator (0 = No, 1 = Yes)
Data Split: Stratified 70% Train (19,530), 15% Validation (4,185), 15% Final Test (4,186)
Strict Isolation: Model selection strictly used Validation metrics. Test set evaluated ONCE.
Feature Set (10 Features):
  - Numerical (6): Age, Academic Pressure, CGPA, Study Satisfaction, Work/Study Hours, Financial Stress
  - Categorical (4): Gender, Sleep Duration, Dietary Habits, Degree
Probability Calibration: CalibratedClassifierCV (method='sigmoid', cv=5 on training data)
