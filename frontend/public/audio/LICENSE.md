## 3. Technical Specifications
- **Format:** 16-bit PCM WAV, 22,050 Hz Mono.
- **Seamless Looping:** Each track features a 500ms half-cosine window crossfade at loop boundaries to prevent click artifacts.
- **Mastering:** Peak amplitude limited strictly to $-0.5\text{ dBFS}$ with dynamic range compression for uniform loudness.
- **Educational Disclaimer:** Ambient music is an optional supportive lifestyle tool and does not provide medical or psychiatric treatment for clinical depression or anxiety.
