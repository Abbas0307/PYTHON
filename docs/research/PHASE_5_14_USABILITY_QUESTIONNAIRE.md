### Official Standardized Scoring Instructions for Researchers (Brooke, 1986)
*(Do not share this formula section with participants)*

The System Usability Scale (SUS) produces a composite score from $0$ to $100$:
1. For odd-numbered items ($1, 3, 5, 7, 9$), the score contribution is:
   $$\text{Contribution} = \text{Scale Position} - 1$$
2. For even-numbered items ($2, 4, 6, 8, 10$), the score contribution is:
   $$\text{Contribution} = 5 - \text{Scale Position}$$
3. Multiply the sum of all 10 contributions by $2.5$:
   $$\text{SUS Total Score} = 2.5 \times \sum_{i=1}^{10} \text{Contribution}_i$$

**Standard Usability Grade Interpretation Benchmarks:**
- **Score $\ge 80.3$:** Grade A (Excellent Usability)
- **Score $68.0 - 80.2$:** Grade B / C (Above Average / Good Acceptability)
- **Score $51.0 - 67.9$:** Grade D (Marginal / Requires Usability Remediation)
- **Score $< 51.0$:** Grade F (Severe Usability Failure / High Rejection Risk)
