### 1. Invitation & Introduction
You are invited to consider participating in a research study evaluating the usability, clarity, and navigation of **Mindora 2.0**. Mindora is an experimental, offline-first digital wellbeing intelligence platform designed specifically for university students.

Before deciding whether to participate, it is essential that you understand why this evaluation is being conducted, what it entails, and how your privacy and voluntary rights are safeguarded. Please take time to read the following information carefully.

---

### 2. What is the Purpose of This Study?
The purpose of this study is purely to evaluate **software usability, comprehension, navigation, and user interface ergonomics**. Specifically, the research team aims to understand:
- Whether students can intuitively navigate the application and understand its educational purpose.
- Whether the visual presentation of lifestyle habits (sleep, study pacing, nourishment, stress friction) is clear and easily understood.
- Whether students find the interactive tools (such as Guided Breathing, Focus Timers, and Action Planners) easy to use without external assistance.
- Whether the privacy and client-side data management controls are transparent and understandable.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **What This Study Does NOT Do:**  
> This study is **not** a clinical trial, psychiatric evaluation, or medical diagnostic assessment. Mindora 2.0 is an educational self-reflection and lifestyle tool. The study does **not** evaluate depression diagnosis, clinical treatment effectiveness, suicide risk prediction, or mental health improvement.

---

### 3. Why Have I Been Invited?
You are being invited because you are currently enrolled as a university or college student aged 18 years or older, and you represent the intended user population of collegiate students who balance academic schedules, study routines, and personal wellbeing.

---

### 4. Do I Have to Take Part?
**No. Participation is entirely voluntary.**  
It is completely up to you whether you decide to take part. If you decide not to participate, or if you decide to withdraw at any point during or after the session, it will have **no impact whatsoever** on your academic standing, grades, relationship with the university, or access to student support services.

---

### 5. What Will Happen If I Take Part?
If you choose to participate, you will take part in a **single, supervised, in-person usability session** lasting approximately **30 to 45 minutes**.

During the session:
1. A trained researcher (moderator) will introduce the study and confirm your informed consent.
2. You will be provided with a standard study testing laptop or tablet with Mindora 2.0 loaded.
3. You will be asked to perform approximately 12 short, realistic tasks (such as exploring the dashboard, creating a study goal, testing a 2-minute breathing timer, and locating privacy deletion settings).
4. You will be encouraged to "think aloud" as you navigate, sharing what you find clear, confusing, or difficult.
5. **Fictional Data Allowed:** You are **never** required to enter your real personal health or psychological details. You may freely use synthetic or fictional scenarios provided by the researcher.
6. At the conclusion of the session, you will be invited to complete a brief 10-item System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire and share open feedback.

---

### 6. Are There Any Discomforts or Risks?
There are no physical risks associated with this software usability study. Potential inconveniences include:
- **Mild Cognitive Effort:** Exploring a new application interface and completing short tasks.
- **Self-Reflection Sensitivity:** The application includes questions about general daily habits (sleep duration, study hours, academic pressure). You are entirely free to enter fictional numbers, skip any question, or pause the session.
- **Immediate Support Access:** In the unlikely event that discussing study stress raises personal distress, verified university and national crisis resources (including 24/7 Tele-MANAS helpline 14416) are permanently accessible on-screen, and the session moderator will provide immediate assistance.

---

### 7. What Are the Benefits?
There are no direct clinical or personal medical benefits to you from participating. However, your feedback will directly contribute to designing clearer, safer, and more accessible privacy-first digital tools for future collegiate students.

---

### 8. Privacy, Confidentiality & Data Management
Your privacy is protected by strict technical and procedural safeguards:
- **Anonymous Identification:** You will be assigned a random, non-identifying code (e.g., `P-001`). Your real name, student ID, email address, or phone number will **never** be linked to your task performance or questionnaire responses.
- **100% Client-Side Local Execution:** Mindora 2.0 operates entirely offline inside the browser. No assessment responses or session data are transmitted over the internet or saved to external servers.
- **No Third-Party AI Services:** Your interactions and usability feedback will **never** be sent to commercial cloud AI models, external LLMs, or analytics trackers.
- **Data Deletion:** Following each evaluation session, the test browser environment is completely wiped using Mindora's built-in Privacy Center and browser cache purge.

---

### 9. What Data is Collected and What is NOT Collected?

| Data Collected by Researchers | Data STRICTLY NOT Collected |
| :--- | :--- |
| Anonymous participant ID code | Student name, institutional ID, or email |
| Task completion status (completed/incomplete) | Personal psychiatric or diagnostic history |
| Time taken to complete each usability task | Real personal depression screening responses |
| Usability questionnaire ratings (SUS scores) | Private student reflection notes |
| Observational notes on UI confusion or errors | Cloud telemetry, IP addresses, or device identifiers |
| Optional audio/screen recording (if explicitly agreed) | Real personal contact numbers or medical records |

---

### 10. Can I Withdraw from the Study?
**Yes, at any time without giving a reason.**  
You can stop the session immediately by informing the moderator. If you withdraw during a session, all observations and notes taken up to that point will be permanently shredded or deleted immediately. After the session, you may withdraw your anonymous data up until the point of aggregate statistical analysis by contacting the research team with your anonymous participant code.
