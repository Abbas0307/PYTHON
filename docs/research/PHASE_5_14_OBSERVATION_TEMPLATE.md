### Usability Defect Severity Classification Standards

| Severity Level | Standard Usability Definition | Engineering Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CRITICAL BLOCKER** | Prevents participant from completing task. No workaround discovered. Interface freezes, crashes, or produces unresolvable dead-end. | Must be remediated before any further student testing. |
| **MAJOR ISSUE** | Causes substantial delay ($> 30\text{ seconds}$), intense frustration, or requires moderator intervention to proceed. | High priority fix before wider usability cohort. |
| **MODERATE ISSUE** | Causes noticeable hesitation, slip, or confusion, but participant recovers independently and completes the task. | Medium priority design polish. |
| **MINOR ISSUE** | Cosmetic awkwardness, minor alignment, subtle typo, or slight visual crowding that does not impede task flow. | Low priority cosmetic enhancement. |
| **SUGGESTION** | Feature enhancement request or aesthetic preference expressed voluntarily by the participant. | Recorded for roadmap prioritization. |
