### Part 5: Risk Assessment & Mitigation Matrix

| Potential Identified Risk | Risk Severity | Implemented Protocol Safeguard & Mitigation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Privacy / Health Stigma Risk:** Participant enters sensitive personal depression or habit data during testing. | **LOW** | **Mitigation:** Protocol explicitly mandates the use of a standardized fictional student profile ("Alex"). Participants are informed they must never enter real health data. |
| **Psychological Distress Risk:** Self-reflection on study stress or depression questions induces emotional discomfort. | **LOW TO MODERATE** | **Mitigation:** Moderator is trained in distress protocols. Crisis resources (Tele-MANAS 14416, Emergency 112) are permanently pinned on-screen and handed out on printed cards. Participant may stop immediately. |
| **Data Leakage Risk:** Testing records or recordings exposed via cloud services or unauthorized access. | **VERY LOW** | **Mitigation:** 100% offline client-side architecture. Full-disk encrypted testing laptops. Complete device wipe between sessions. Master consent locked separately. |
| **Misunderstanding Software Scope:** Student believes Mindora 2.0 diagnosed them with depression. | **LOW** | **Mitigation:** Prominent non-diagnostic framing on Welcome screen, survey results, and dashboard. Explicit debriefing confirming the tool is educational only. |
