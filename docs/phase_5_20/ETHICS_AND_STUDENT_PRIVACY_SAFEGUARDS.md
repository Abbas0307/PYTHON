## 1. Ethical Governance Principles

Mindora 2.0 is built on an unconditional commitment to student dignity, psychological autonomy, and privacy. The platform is an educational self-reflection and lifestyle intelligence tool, strictly non-clinical, and engineered to prevent surveillance or coercion.

### Six Ethical Pillars:
1. **Voluntary Participation:** Self-assessment is 100% voluntary. No academic credit, institutional evaluation, or administrative access is conditioned upon using Mindora.
2. **Explicit Affirmative Consent:** Consent checkboxes are never pre-checked. Terms of service, privacy policies, and guardian support alerts require separate, conscious student authorization.
3. **Zero Clinical Inferences to Third Parties:** Parents, guardians, university staff, or peers can never receive PHQ-9 answers, psychological labels, or severity tags.
4. **Minor Student Safeguarding:** Students under 18 cannot activate automatic notification workflows without verified parental authorization.
5. **Instant Consent Revocation:** A single click instantly wipes guardian contact permissions and disables any automated alert rules.
6. **Zero Commercial Data Exploitation:** Mindora contains zero ad trackers, zero telemetry analytics, and zero commercial data broker pipelines.

---

## 2. Institutional Ethics & Regulatory Alignment

- **FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act):** Protected educational and counseling records remain student-controlled.
- **GDPR / UK GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** Right to erasure (Article 17) and Data Protection by Design and by Default (Article 25) fully implemented in the Privacy Data Center.
- **DPDP Act 2023 (India Digital Personal Data Protection Act):** Verifiable parental consent enforced for minor accounts; unambiguous purpose limitation maintained.

---

## 3. Human Participant Testing Prohibition

Consistent with Phase 5.15 and 5.16 governance rules:
- Real student human participants are NOT recruited or subjected to testing during this phase.
- All testing utilized synthetic fixtures and validated test harnesses.
- Human trials require formal institutional ethics board (IRB / IEC) protocol approval prior to deployment.
